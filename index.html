ABOUT

Technical, but not only technical.

I'm studying Computer Science at Virginia Tech, with minors in Finance and Viticulture. I'm interested in building things, telling stories, and following my curiosity wherever it leads.

I've always liked trying things just to see where they go. That's led me to build software, work on research, explore machine learning, and now work on a startup. I enjoy documenting my experiences through writing, and I'm especially drawn to people, places, and experiences that challenge the way I think and leave me inspired.
